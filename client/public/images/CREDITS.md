# Image credits and permissions

## Photos in use

The site owner supplied these photos on 23 October 2026 and confirmed in writing the same
day: "I have permission to use the images I supplied." Keep the written permission (from
the photographer, the organisation shown, or the image library licence) on file, because
several photos show identifiable children and young people.

None of these photos show the foundation's own staff, beneficiaries or events. Alt text and
captions describe only what is visible and never say the scene is a Lead Vest Foundation
activity. Replace them with the foundation's own photos (with documented guardian consent)
as soon as those exist.
